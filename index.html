One platform

One chassis.
Endless cars.

Every RDM is built on the same bolt-together platform. Modules connect with standard mounts and a single data plug, so an upgrade is a service appointment — not a new car.

  1. PowertrainPetrol, hybrid or electric. Same mounts, swapped in about two hours.
  2. Energy floorFuel tank or battery pack slides into the flat floor.
  3. InteriorSeats, dash and screen lift out as one tray. Go from 5 seats to cargo.
  4. CornersWheels, brakes and suspension per corner. City comfort or gravel ready.
  5. FasciasFront and rear bumpers with lights. Refresh the face, not the car.
  6. RoofSteel, panoramic glass, rails or solar. Six bolts, one afternoon.
