One platform

One chassis.
Endless cars.

Every RDM is built on the same bolt-together platform. Modules connect with standard mounts and a single data plug, so an upgrade is a service appointment — not a new car.

  1. PowertrainPetrol, hybrid or electric. Same mounts, swapped in about two hours.
  2. Energy floorFuel tank or battery pack slides into the flat floor.
  3. InteriorSeats, dash and screen lift out as one tray. Go from 5 seats to cargo.
  4. CornersWheels, brakes and suspension per corner. City comfort or gravel ready.
  5. FasciasFront and rear bumpers with lights. Refresh the face, not the car.
  6. RoofSteel, panoramic glass, rails or solar. Six bolts, one afternoon.

Upgrade, don't replace

Your car,
version two.

Most cars are thrown away for one tired part. With RDM you swap that part, hand in the old module for refurbishment, and drive out the same day.

  1. 2026

    Start simple

    Buy an RDM 1989 with the R1 Turbo petrol module. Simple, light and easy to live with — first car energy.

  2. 2029

    Go hybrid

    Longer commute? Swap to the H2 Hybrid. Your R1 goes back to Radom to be refurbished.

  3. 2032

    Go electric

    Charging is everywhere now. Drop in the E3 module and energy floor — same body, same seats, same memories.

  4. 2035

    Refresh the face

    New fascias, new lights, new wheels. It looks like a 2035 car because, mostly, it is one.
